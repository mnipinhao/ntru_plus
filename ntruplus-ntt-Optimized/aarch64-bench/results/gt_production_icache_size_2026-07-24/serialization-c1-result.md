## Interpretation

The code-size reduction is real and does not require changing the public byte
contract or polynomial arithmetic. The generic and CQ frontends remain
layout-specific; only already-proven shared packing work is compacted.

The result also corrects the initial estimate:

- whole-endpoint merging is unnecessary;
- generic pack should use the existing 84-instruction compact scheduled core;
- CQ keygen should use its existing frontend with the exact 60-instruction
  production pack core;
- QSoA decap serialization can remain unchanged in this wave.

No unpack or decapsulation-verification redesign was required for promotion.
