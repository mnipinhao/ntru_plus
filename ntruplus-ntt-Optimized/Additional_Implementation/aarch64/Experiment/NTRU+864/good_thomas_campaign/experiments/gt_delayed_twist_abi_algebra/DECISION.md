# M5T-A decision

Status: **reject** for the proposed scalar delayed-twist ABI.

The basis proof establishes that omitted `lambda^s` cannot be represented by
`(d,pi)` leaf metadata and therefore cannot be repaired by `d^-2` in BaseMul.
The verified pure row-rotation subfamily is retained in the shared ledger as
layout/orientation freedom, not as a separate optimization candidate.
