# D1-P3B17 FromBytes remaining-cost hypothesis

P3B11 measures 935.717 cycles against C1, while the remaining gap to Official
was inferred across older experiments.  This gate directly measures Official
`stock_from` and P3B11 in one binary and PMU campaign.  Both receive identical
1296 arbitrary bytes and produce their native layouts; exact composed-map
equality is checked before timing.
