# M5R: full-register Pass-2 DAG experiment

M5R tests whether the eight ABI callee-saved Neon registers are more valuable
inside the GT core than the cost of preserving their low halves once in the
public wrapper.  It is an `existing_region_replacement` of M5M's exact
633-instruction one-bank region.

Two candidates were solved remotely with Slothy 0.2.2 on
`pinhao@172.25.166.141:51208` using the N1 scheduling proxy:

1. M5R-A deletes thirteen explicit `orr` preservation copies.  The resulting
   620-instruction DAG is OPTIMAL under functional-only RA with no spill.
2. M5R-B additionally hoists `bitrev3`, `q`, and the packed NTT9 roots into
   fixed `v13`, `v14`, and `v15`.  Its 617-instruction bank is also OPTIMAL,
   no-spill, and completes real-instruction split-window scheduling.

The integrated six-bank wrapper loads those three common constants once,
calls the same 617-instruction helper six times, stores each bank's eighteen
FR-0 vectors immediately, and introduces no coefficient memory boundary.
The public full-Forward wrapper saves/restores `d8-d15` once.

`make check` runs the static ledger, 1,122 Pass-2 oracle cases, 1,254 full
Forward differential cases, and an explicit `d8-d15` ABI probe.  Pi 5 paired
PMU evidence is generated by `python3 run_pi5.py` and retained under the
ignored `build/pi5-formal` directory; its summary is transcribed in
`results.md`.
